 -<Main Help>-
  welcome to online sustingus shop!
  to continue, please either login to an existing account,
  or create a new one with register.
  if you cannot do either of those, then contact mudkip
  or tilo for further assistance.
  [L][Login Help]
  [R][Register help]
  [Left Control][Back]
 
 -<Login Help>-
  Enter a username and password to log in.
  there isnt much else to it!
  [Left Control][Back]
  
 -<Register Help>-
  Username: a name used to identify you/login with.
  Password: a series of characters required with a username
   to access your account, make it something complex and 
    rememberable.
  Home mail address: the address of a frogport to be used
   as your personal mailbox. this is where orders will be
    sent by default, unless you have public frogports on.
  Use public frogports: turn this on to be able to recieve
   orders at public frogports. The one used automatically
    changes depending on which one's closer.
  [Left Control][Back]
